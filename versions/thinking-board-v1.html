01 · Absorb the brief

What they asked vs. what they're grading

Read the brief twice: once for the task, once for the hidden rubric.

DesignProduct
THE ASK

Design the SuprFleet web app of 2030

How an operator glances at, understands, questions, and acts on the whole fleet. Lead with the answer, the why and the action, not the data. An incremental restyle is a fail.

THE USER

Distrustful, interrupted, time-pressed

Runs 2 to 500 vehicles. Ops, maintenance, service and accounts teams at scale. Utilisation is money; cost leaks in a hundred places.

Time-to-value for a new user is the one metric they named.
Layers in order: concept → IA → interaction → visual.
Show divergence: several real directions before picking one.
AI you can trust: question and override, not just consume.
Tier = autonomy. The ladder is the business model.
Hard states: empty, loading, error, 500 vehicles.
Depth over breadth. 8–12 hours. One surface, brilliantly.
Indian commercial fleets. Not a Samsara clone.

The real test: can we turn dense telemetry into an answer an operator trusts, and show the thinking that got us there.

GAP 1

Chat is table stakes

Samsara Assistant, Motive Atlas, Geotab Ace. "Ask your fleet" is no longer innovation.

GAP 2

No evidence, no confidence

AI outputs don't show why. That's the distrustful operator's whole objection.

GAP 3

Autonomy is binary

Alert or automated workflow. No visible, graduated ladder with guardrails.

GAP 4

Events, not ₹

Tools report what happened. Operators think in money and utilisation.

GAP 5

One size for 2 and 500

Same dense UI whether you own 2 trucks or run 500.

OUR EDGE

Bytebeam's depth

200+ device parameters, OTA, remote diagnostics. OEM-grade data, not just a GPS box.

Say it in the room: "I didn't look for features to copy. I looked for what every competitor still gets wrong."

One job: "Keep the fleet earning, and only pull me in when a decision needs my judgement or my money."

Today: 4 tools, 300+ alerts a day, leads calling him to approve small things, leaks found at month-end close. Distrust: "92%? Says who? Show me."

07 · Reference model

Run the fleet like a hospital runs care

Not "a vehicle is a body" (Intangles already does that). A hospital keeps hundreds of patients safe with a few senior people, through triage, early-warning scores and delegated authority. That is Vikram's problem.

DIRECTION A · CHOSEN

Hospital / care system

Maps 1:1 to the ladder. Explains trust and delegation.

DIRECTION B · BORROW

Aviation dark cockpit

If nothing's wrong, nothing lights up. We take "quiet when well".

DIRECTION C · BORROW

Investment portfolio

Each vehicle is an earning asset. We take ₹ as the unit of every answer.

Guardrail: the hospital is the mental model, never the skin. No heartbeat lines, no red crosses.

08 · System loops

Break the alarm-fatigue loop, feed the trust loop

In ICUs, most monitor alarms are non-actionable (studies cite 72–99%), so staff stop responding. Today's 300 fleet alerts a day do the same.

Systems

  C --> D[Real issue missed]
  D --> E[Money lost]

SUPRFLEET · VIRTUOUS LOOP 
flowchart LR

  B --> C[Outcome + feedback logged]
  C --> D[Confidence rises]
  D --> E[Becomes a standing order]
  E --> F[Fewer cases need a human]
  F --> A

Override isn't failure; it's training data. Every "this is wrong because…" makes the next answer better and the next rung safer.

09 · Configuration layer

Standing Orders: the system acts, money and risk come to a human

Set at onboarding, editable any time. Three questions decide every action.

DesignProductSystems
flowchart LR
  S([Case detected]) --> Q1{Safety or legal?}
  Q1 -- yes --> E[Escalate now: call owner, prep playbook]
  Q1 -- no --> Q2{Costs money?}
  Q2 -- no --> Q3{Reversible?}
  Q3 -- yes --> A[Auto: handle and log]
  Q3 -- no --> K[Ask for approval]
  Q2 -- yes --> Q4{Within budget?}
  Q4 -- yes --> I[Auto, then inform with undo]
  Q4 -- no --> K

Auto no human

Notify driver, raise ticket, reschedule reminder, push a non-critical update overnight.

Auto + inform undo window

Service under ₹5,000, pre-order a tyre, book a charging slot.

Ask approval

Major repair, swap vehicle mid-trip, immobilise, driver suspension.

Escalate now

Accident, confirmed theft, compliance breach.

After Vikram approves the same suggestion 5 times: "Make this a standing order?" That's how a fleet climbs from rung 2 to rung 3.

12 · Release roadmap

Each release climbs one rung, and proves the next one is safe

ProductSystems
MVP · RUNG 0–1

Answers you can trust

  • Fleet Pulse: 5 vitals + the one answer
  • Cases ranked by ₹ × urgency
  • Diagnose with evidence strip + confidence
  • One-click deterministic actions
  • Approval gate for spend
  • Empty / loading / offline states

Proves: time to first useful answer < 10 min. Weekly active operators. Cases opened vs ignored.

PHASE 1 · RUNG 2

The system suggests

  • Corrective SOPs on each case
  • Question / override the AI
  • Standing Orders (spend limits, escalation)
  • Ask, answered as cards
  • Vehicle Record
  • Assign to team

Proves: suggestion acceptance rate, override rate falling, ₹ leakage recovered.

PHASE 2 · RUNG 3

The system acts within limits

  • Autonomous guardrails
  • "Make this a standing order?"
  • Root-cause grouping (60 cases → 7)
  • Fleet Health cohorts
  • Audit trail of system actions + undo

Proves: % cases auto-handled, undo rate low, upgrade to higher tier.

2030 VISION · RUNG 4

Fleet on autopilot

  • Goal-based operation ("keep cost/km under ₹20")
  • Policy simulation on past data
  • Health forecasts & true vehicle age
  • Voice / WhatsApp companion

Proves: human minutes per vehicle per week trend to near zero.
